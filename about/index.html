About

Christine Warner

Hey there! I’m Christine Warner, founder of Crackerjack Solutions.

After 20+ years working across corporate operations in diverse industries, I’ve seen every flavor of business chaos imaginable. I’ve spent the better part of a decade figuring out how to fix it — for myself and for my clients.

What I actually do:

I take the tools, systems, and workflows that are running your business on chaos and turn them into one clean, connected operation. Less manual work. More automation. A client experience your clients will actually rave about.

I specialize in Moxie CRM — the all-in-one platform built for solo service providers — and I’ve helped coaches and freelancers go from scattered spreadsheets to systems that practically run themselves.

What I believe:

That your business systems should work as hard as you do. That integrity isn’t optional — it’s the foundation. And that the best technology is the kind you barely notice because it’s just quietly running in the background, making everything smoother.

When I’m not helping clients untangle their tech, you’ll find me exploring the trails near Traverse City, tending my garden, getting lost in a good book, or working on my next painting. Even a systems person needs to recharge. 🌿

Matthew Fisher

Four years ago, Matthew came into my world with zero technical skills and no clear direction. I saw potential in him — the kind you just know is there — and pointed him down the road toward web development. He took that nudge and ran with it, teaching himself everything from scratch.

Today, Matthew is the web development half of Crackerjack Solutions. When clients need a beautiful, functional website to go along with their streamlined systems — Matthew makes it happen.

I’m Matthew, lead web designer here at Crackerjack Solutions. With a strong foundation in web design principles and a certification to back it up, I’m dedicated to helping businesses and individuals establish their online presence.

I specialize in building and maintaining websites using WordPress, the world’s most popular content management system. While I may be early in my professional journey, my commitment to continuous learning and improvement has equipped me with the skills needed to deliver top-notch web solutions.

Whether you’re looking for a brand-new website or need help revamping an existing one, I’m here to make the process smooth and enjoyable.

When I’m not busy designing websites, you can find me exploring the latest technologies, keeping up with current events, or enjoying music or nature.

I’m always excited to take on new challenges and help my clients succeed in the digital world.
