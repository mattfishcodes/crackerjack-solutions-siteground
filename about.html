About

Christine Warner

Hey there! I’m Christine Warner, founder of Crackerjack Solutions. After 20+ years working across corporate operations in diverse industries, I’ve seen every flavor of business chaos imaginable. I’ve spent the better part of a decade figuring out how to fix it — for myself and for my clients. What I actually do: I take the tools, systems, and workflows that are running your business on chaos and turn them into one clean, connected operation. Less manual work. More automation. A client experience your clients will actually rave about. I specialize in Moxie CRM — the all-in-one platform built for solo service providers — and I’ve helped coaches and freelancers go from scattered spreadsheets to systems that practically run themselves. What I believe: That your business systems should work as hard as you do. That integrity isn’t optional — it’s the foundation. And that the best technology is the kind you barely notice because it’s just quietly running in the background, making everything smoother. When I’m not helping clients untangle their tech, you’ll find me exploring the trails near Traverse City, tending my garden, getting lost in a good book, or working on my next painting. Even a systems person needs to recharge. 🌿
